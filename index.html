With FlowDocs, they send one link: the client opens it on any device, reviews the contract, signs it and pays the deposit right after signing, so there is no separate invoice to chase. The freelancer is notified when the contract is signed and paid.

Are FlowDocs e-signatures legally valid in India?

India recognises electronic signatures under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and FlowDocs captures each signature with the signer's explicit consent. Every signature is timestamped and an audit trail PDF is kept with the signed document. Some document types, such as wills and property sale deeds, are excluded from electronic signing under the Act.

Which payment methods are supported?

Clients pay through Razorpay with UPI, cards, net banking or wallets when the freelancer has connected Razorpay. Without Razorpay, the sign page shows the freelancer's UPI ID and bank transfer details, and the freelancer confirms the payment.
